Path extraction and sparse path-Jaccard functions derive from the preserved
02_revision-simulation/R/mlsas_optimized.R in the ML-SAS research repository.
Those functions are retained in R/path_engine.R. Addcl1 and normalization follow
the empirical pipeline. Total inference follows 05_empirical-study/R/mlsas_inference.R;
conditional inference follows rerun_conditional_local_inference.R. Package wrappers,
streaming accumulation, input validation, spatial constructors, documentation and
small teaching examples were added for this release. The source paper and
frozen research snapshot were not changed. Package examples are small synthetic teaching datasets.

licenses. No dependency code or original DGP-generation source is vendored.
